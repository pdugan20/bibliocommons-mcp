## What's checked out

> What do I have out, and what's due soon?

[`list_loans`](/reference/mcp-tools) returns your current checkouts with due
dates, renewal counts, and the actions each item supports.

## Renew

> Renew everything that's due this week.

[`renew_loan`](/reference/mcp-tools) renews one or more checkouts in a single
call — pass `checkout_ids` (from `list_loans`) and an optional `dry_run` to
preview. The result mirrors the loans view with the new due dates and
incremented renewal counts; a `failures` map captures anything that couldn't
renew (e.g. another patron has it on hold).

## Return a digital item early

See [Borrow & return digital](/guides/digital) — `check_in_loan` returns a
digital checkout before its due date. Physical items return at the branch.
