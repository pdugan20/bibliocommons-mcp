For **physical** items (books, CDs, DVDs). For digital titles see
[Borrow & return digital](/guides/digital).

## Place a hold

> Place a hold on _Plastic Eternity_ at Central.

[`place_hold`](/reference/mcp-tools) takes `bib_ids` (a list — pass one for the
common case) and an optional `pickup_branch` (name or 3-letter code; falls back
to your `default_pickup_branch`). There's no batch endpoint, so multiple holds
are placed sequentially with a small `delay_seconds` between each; per-item
failures don't stop the run, and the result splits `placed` vs `failures`.

Common failure causes: you already have the item on hold (409), it isn't
holdable (reference-only), or a transient gateway error.

## See your holds

- [`list_holds`](/reference/mcp-tools) — everything you're waiting on, with queue
  position (1 = front of the line).
- [`ready_for_pickup`](/reference/mcp-tools) — just the ones waiting for you.

## Cancel a hold

<Warning>
  Cancelling is **irreversible** — you lose your queue position. Confirm before
  running it.
</Warning>

[`cancel_hold`](/reference/mcp-tools) takes a list of `{hold_id, bib_id}` refs
(get them from `list_holds`) and a `dry_run` flag to preview without cancelling.
It's marked **destructive**, so well-behaved clients prompt before calling.

> What am I waiting on? … okay, cancel the hold on the DVD.
