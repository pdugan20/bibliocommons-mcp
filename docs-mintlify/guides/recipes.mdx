The interface is conversation, so the best "API" is a good prompt. Here are
starting points; Claude picks the right tool and fills in the parameters.

## Discover

- "Find the new Mudhoney album on CD."
- "Search for ebooks about urban beekeeping, newest first."
- "Is _The Fifth Season_ available at Lake City right now?"

## Holds

- "Place a hold on _Plastic Eternity_ at Central."
- "What am I waiting on, and what's my place in line?"
- "What's ready for me to pick up?"
- "Cancel the hold on the DVD." _(it'll confirm; cancelling loses your spot)_

## Digital

- "Borrow the ebook of _The Fifth Season_."
- "Get me on the waitlist for the _Stone Sky_ audiobook."
- "Return the ebook I just finished."

## Loans & account

- "What do I have checked out and when is it due?"
- "Renew everything due this week."
- "Is the catalog working? Check my hold quotas." _(runs `library_health`)_

<Note>
  Combine steps: "Find the new Sally Rooney novel, and if Central has a copy,
  place a hold there." Claude chains `search` → `availability` → `place_hold`.
</Note>
