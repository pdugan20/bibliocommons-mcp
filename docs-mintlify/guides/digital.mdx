---
title: Borrow & return digital
description: Check out available e-books and audiobooks, join the Libby waitlist, and return early.
---

Digital titles (e-books, downloadable audio) flow through Libby/OverDrive. Three
tools, depending on availability.

## Borrow an available title

> Borrow the ebook of _The Fifth Season_.

[`borrow_digital`](/reference/mcp-tools) checks out an **available-now** digital
item immediately (it's a checkout, not a hold). Takes `bib_ids` (a list).
Confirm availability first with [`availability`](/guides/branches) if unsure.

## Join the waitlist (unavailable titles)

> Get in line for the audiobook of _The Stone Sky_.

[`place_digital_hold`](/reference/mcp-tools) joins the Libby waitlist for a title
with no copies available. It needs a **notification email** —
`digital_notification_email` in your [config](/reference/configuration) (the
same address your BiblioCommons account uses) — and counts against your digital
hold quota (see `library_health`).

## Return early

> Return the ebook I just borrowed.

[`check_in_loan`](/reference/mcp-tools) returns a digital checkout before its due
date (the "Return now" button). Takes a list of `{checkout_id, metadata_id}`
refs (from [`list_loans`](/guides/loans)) and a `dry_run` flag. Only digital
items can be checked in this way — physical items return at the branch.

<Note>
  Physical vs digital use different endpoints under the hood, but you don't have
  to think about it — ask for what you want and the right tool runs.
</Note>
